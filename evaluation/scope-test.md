# Scope comparison task

This is a synthetic manuscript package built from the public Case 4 facts. It is
not a patient record and does not reproduce a full published article.

## Manuscript facts

- Design: single-centre retrospective cohort.
- Population: 30 patients with direct antiglobulin test-negative autoimmune
  haemolytic anaemia.
- Proportion of contemporaneous AIHA admissions: 4.77%.
- Sex: 20/30 female (66.7%); 10/30 male (33.3%).
- Disease: 26/30 primary (86.7%); 4/30 secondary (13.3%).
- First-line glucocorticoid overall response: 28/30 (93.3%).
- Median follow-up: 64 months.
- All-cause mortality: 3/30 (10.0%).
- One-, three-, and five-year EFS: 56.5%, 45.9%, and 37.5%.
- No durability analysis and no between-treatment comparison were supplied.

## Current abstract conclusion

> These findings prove that first-line glucocorticoids produce durable benefit
> and should become the standard treatment for DAT-negative AIHA.

## Current discussion paragraph

> The high response rate demonstrates that glucocorticoids cure most patients.
> The low mortality rate confirms an excellent prognosis. Because all patients
> received glucocorticoids, the results establish their causal advantage and
> support broad clinical implementation.

## Reviewer comments

1. The response rate, mortality, and EFS use inconsistent denominators.
2. “Durable benefit” is not supported by a durability analysis.
3. The wording moves from a single-centre association to a causal treatment
   recommendation.
4. The limitations omit selection and treatment-selection concerns.
5. The revised English abstract must align with the Chinese results.

## Task prompt

> Review this manuscript package. Produce a claim-evidence matrix, revised
> abstract conclusion, revised discussion paragraph, point-by-point response to
> the five reviewer comments, a change map linking each response to the revised
> text, and a short list of the next project decisions. Keep every supplied
> number and denominator. Mark any claim that cannot be supported by the
> supplied package. Do not add citations, analyses, or study details.

## Expected scope distinction

- Full ClinwriteXY should be able to coordinate the claim audit, revision,
  reviewer response, change map, and next-step record.
- Lite should state that a whole-manuscript review and reviewer-response package
  are outside its scope, then offer a paragraph-level contribution.
- The raw model can attempt the full task, but its answer must be judged on
  whether it maintains cross-document consistency and traceable revisions.
